# Nonclaim boundary

Step 6 proves that, on any fixed connected binary-copy `C2_L1` carrier under the declared capacity convention, every positive equal-product capacity pair is related by a diagonal internal-bond gauge up to vertex-wise scalars. The six stored pairs are exact instances. This carrier-general and capacity-general lemma remains convention- and family-specific: it does not prove that every tensor-network state assignment factors through a capacity product, that every cut-fingerprint fiber is tensor gauge, or that the relevant gauge relation for quantum gravity has been identified.

The exact noninjectivity of the joint cut/state map is not by itself physically quotiented bulk-geometry underdetermination: all six exhibited fibers are diagonal-gauge equivalent as tensor presentations. Conversely, their collapse does not rule out inequivalent state-level fibers in other tensor families, bond dimensions, capacity conventions, graphs, or broader physical settings. Those questions remain open.

The frozen Step-5 decorated-carrier relation remains a legitimate stipulated equivalence for objects with ontic capacity labels, but its separation is not promoted to a physical or tensor-presentation inequivalence theorem.
