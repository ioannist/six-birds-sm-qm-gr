**To Kill Three Stones with Six Birds: A Common Grammar for the SM, QM, and GR**

## Abstract (draft)

A standing question in foundations is whether the deep problems of distinct domains — *why* the Standard Model has the
structure it does, and *why* quantum mechanics and general relativity fail to combine — share any common explanatory
machinery, or are merely unrelated. We report that a single, frozen, **layer-agnostic structural calculus** — a catalog of
substrate-independent laws governing how one theory emerges as a quotient/closure of another (the Six Birds emergence
calculus) — produces foundational-grade results on both, and, decisively, yields **three forced, falsifiable,
mainstream-contradicting predictions.**

Applied under an active anti-contamination guard on finite, audited, reproducible toy carriers, the same calculus gives
the two **opposite result-genres its own laws anticipate in advance**. For the Standard Model, a *demarcation* that proves
which features are structurally forced versus irreducibly observed-input — recovering, non-circularly, the grand-unified
embedding ratios `sin²θ_W = 3/8` and `k_Y = 5/3`, and unifying proton-stability, monopole-absence, and color-separation as
one structural object. For QM–GR, a *unification* that recognizes Ryu–Takayanagi holographic entanglement as a special
case of a single ledger / shadow-price duality (a recovery checked in a separate adversarial review pass).

The framework's distinctive output is its three predictions, each contradicting a prevailing program. **(i)** Boundary
entanglement does *not* determine the bulk geometry: the entanglement→geometry map carries a robust kernel, so strong
"it-from-qubit" — complete entanglement-based bulk reconstruction — **fails**; falsifiable by exhibiting a non-trivial
holographic geometry whose full entanglement fingerprint is injective. **(ii)** Gravity does **not** mediate
entanglement: its channel is forced to be classically-indexed, so BMV-class experiments are predicted **null** —
gravitational decoherence without entanglement. **(iii)** The capacity to bear stable records is
structurally tied to baryon-number conservation: a record-bearing universe **forbids proton decay and magnetic
monopoles**, contradicting grand unification; falsifiable by observing either.

Every result is computed under adversarial self-correction — tautologies, circularities, and rigging toward *both* novelty
and recovery were detected and rejected, and surviving claims independently recomputed against explicit can-fail controls;
two construction results were stress-tested in adversarial review cycles and settled. We claim **no frame-transfer**: the results are
conditional, toy-level structural facts, with *n = 2* on the universality claim and the all-structures theorem-upgrades
open. What we establish is a proof of principle — that one layer-agnostic structural grammar can make foundational,
experimentally-falsifiable, mainstream-contradicting contributions across physics' deepest divides — and we place its three
predictions before the community to test.

## Positioning notes (the angle, and how to land it)

**The angle in one sentence.** *One frozen, substrate-independent structural calculus, pointed at the two most unrelated
deep problems in physics, produces opposite-genre foundational results it predicts in advance — and, crucially, three
falsifiable predictions that contradict strong it-from-qubit, quantized-mediator gravity, and grand unification.*

**What makes it a physics paper, not a philosophy paper.** The three falsifiable, experimentally-contradictable predictions.
Lead with them. They are the teeth a referee and an experimentalist can grab; without them this is "an interesting
formalism." With them it is a framework that *sticks its neck out*.

**The credibility ladder (order of grab-ability for a skeptical reader):**
1. **Three falsifiable, contrarian predictions** (it-from-qubit fails; BMV is null; proton decay/monopoles forbidden) — the hook.
2. **A review-checked recovery of a landmark result** (RT/holographic entanglement as one ledger law) — proves the
   calculus is not vacuous and earns the right to be heard; *two* construction results passed adversarial review cycles.
3. **The unifying thesis** (one grammar → two problems → opposite predicted genres) — the over-arching claim.
4. **The method** (finite audited toys + adversarial self-correction + symmetric scrutiny) — the discipline that lets a
   reader trust the predictions weren't fitted.

**How to defuse the "fringe overclaim" reflex (this framework is unconventional — honesty is load-bearing for impact):**
state the bounds *inside* the abstract, not as an afterthought — finite-toy, conditional, frame-transfer unproven, *n = 2*,
enumeration-strength where it applies. The combination *bold falsifiable predictions + scrupulous, pre-emptive honesty* is
the thing that can make this land rather than be dismissed. Overclaiming kills it faster than under-claiming.

**Target communities (the abstract is written to be legible to all four):** quantum-gravity / holography (the it-from-qubit
prediction, the RT subsumption); particle physics / GUTs (the proton-decay prediction); quantum foundations (Bell /
measurement, classified by the same calculus in the body); and foundations-of-physics / emergence (the meta-theory).

**What to keep OUT of the abstract (avoid the deflation *and* the inflation traps):**
- Do **not** hedge the predictions into "recovery" — they are forced, falsifiable, and contrarian; say so.
- Do **not** inflate to claims about *nature* — these are toy-level structural facts that *predict* and are *falsifiable*,
  not proven about the world.
- Do **not** lead with internal jargon (GROUND-landing, Δ_fact, F-numbers) — describe operationally; the names live in the
  body.

**Open thread for the intro/discussion (not the abstract):** the same calculus also classifies Bell nonlocality
(the carrier is not a local hidden variable), black-hole information (no physical loss at the full holographic boundary),
and the cosmological constant (no in-layer value-law) under one defect/obstruction/moduli machinery — breadth evidence for
the universality claim, supporting the three headline predictions.
