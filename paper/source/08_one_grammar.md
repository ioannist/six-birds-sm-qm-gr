# What the two tracks share

Both tracks use the same formal vocabulary: quotients, closure, factorization defects, common refinements, and graded
results. That reuse is real, and it is useful for organizing finite constructions. It does not by itself support
stronger statements: that the grammar *forces* physics across domains, that it reaches a foundational grade, or
that it is universal. Stronger ties would need constructions that are not available here: a grounding of record
stability, a derived gravitational record channel, a non-commuting pair of routes, or one composition law shared by
Born and area ledgers.

## Keeping the tracks separate, and the limits of that

The SM construction was typed as a selection layer and the QM–GR construction as a common-refinement problem.
Validators rejected certain cross-track tokens, and the build history contains genuine self-corrections. This is
evidence of procedural separation, not of mathematical independence or blinding. The protocol was designed and
reported by the same program, an earlier external gate on the missing-layer atlas never issued final approval, and
the atlases are not independent preregistration evidence. A formal object that recurs in both tracks is therefore a
comparison datum, not proof that nature realizes a substrate-independent law.

## An inventory of shared objects

| formal object | Standard-Model track | QM–GR track |
| --- | --- | --- |
| factorization through a readout | clean/breaking classification of branches on the finite carrier | exact incomparability of the declared coordinate readouts; no commutator interpretation |
| common refinement | the regular $SU(5)$ embedding constructs hypercharge and coset roles under named inputs | the abstract carrier $L$ factors both readouts by construction; field provenance is conditional |
| record vocabulary | bounded censuses of candidate invariant channels; the implication is token-definition-sensitive | an assumed perfect-record channel is LOCC; record formation is not derived |
| value and moduli diagnostics | the F47 surface is convention-sensitive and selects no measured scale | every sampled F50 background has a fixed point; raw-map stability is an optional criterion, not a $\Lambda$ law |
| finite graph duality | no cross-track identity | exact min-cut primal/dual certificates and shadow prices; entropy saturation only sampled |

The table supports a modest statement: the same typed questions expose analogous failure modes on two deliberately
different finite substrates. It does not identify the physical mechanisms. In particular:

- the $SU(5)$ $X/Y$ coset has several computed roles inside one constructed frame, but it is not the QM--GR
   factorization defect and does not explain perturbative non-renormalizability;
- record candidates in the gauge carrier and a classical-record quantum channel share terminology, but no map
   connects them and neither forces the other;
- commuting diagrams of the F51 shape organize both an embedding and an access fork, but this formal resemblance
   does not select $SU(5)$, prove that the QM–GR parent is unique, or establish a universal composition law.

## The one-grammar statement, bounded

What can be claimed across the tracks is this:

> One frozen structural vocabulary was instantiated on two finite, differently typed carriers. It produced several sound
> conditional constructions, two genuine toy theorems, and useful diagnoses of where stronger claims fail. The
> recurrence is a methodological datum with $n=2$. It neither demonstrates universality nor transfers any statement to
> nature.

The bound is what makes the negative findings informative rather than embarrassing. Exact quotienting shows that the
naive P1 kernel was a reparametrization, and P1's two finite subproblems are then completed at a precisely stated
strength: forward-reachability disjointness for nineteen graph pairs, and gauge collapse of the six state-level pairs.
Explicit channel analysis isolates the missing dynamical premise of P2, and a broader invariant census exposes P3's
dependence on the definition of a record. P2 and P3 remain construction programs that require gravitational dynamics
and invariant-ring and record machinery not supplied here.

Read as a whole, the development recorded in Appendix C is a
bounded example of theory repair guided by the calculus: typed failures led to reconstructed carriers, sharper theorem
objects, exact countermodels, and precise successor requirements. It proposes no new physical theory of the SM, QM,
or GR. What it demonstrates is a workflow for engineering and auditing emergence claims, at the scale of one research
package.

## Limits

- Both carrier families are finite and deliberately capped; there is no continuum limit and no transfer to nature.
- The separation of the tracks is procedural self-report, not independent blinding.
- Recognition results keep every named input, in particular the regular $SU(5)$ embedding and the graph and
   tensor-network model.
- The results passed an internal adversarial claims audit with machine-verifiable validators; that is not journal
   review or independent replication.
- The missing-layer and unification atlases have unresolved limitations as public support
   (Section 9 and Appendix B).
