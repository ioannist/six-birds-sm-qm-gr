\begin{abstract}
We present a common finite constructive grammar -- six structural primitives built from quotients, closures,
obstructions, and common refinements -- and instantiate it on two deliberately different problem domains: selection of
Standard-Model gauge structure and the relation between quantum and gravitational descriptions. On the Standard-Model
track, a conjugation-consistent finite carrier of \(1{,}066\) genuinely chiral gauge structures yields a branch-typed
selection: within the declared representation, charge, field, and singleton/two-scalar caps, \(2|3\) is the unique
carrier orbit/dimension family admitting a clean breaking branch under the inherited \(SU(N)\to SU(N-1)\) residual
proxy, every clean branch is \(SU(2)\)-active, and every \(SU(4)\) branch is breaking. Exact construction of the
regular \(SU(5)\) embedding derives its one-dimensional hypercharge direction and recovers
\(\sin^2\theta_W=3/8\) and \(k_Y=5/3\), conditional on the embedding, the \(\overline{\mathbf 5}+\mathbf{10}\) package,
and the weak-pair normalization. On the QM--GR track, an abstract access carrier yields an exact incomparability
theorem for the declared QM and GR coordinate partitions, and contracted-state entropies, graph cuts, monogamy
controls, and an explicit max-flow/min-cut dual certificate support a finite-graph recognition of the identity
\(\operatorname{Area}(\text{min cut})=\sum_e c_e y_e\), with \(y_e\) the edge shadow prices. Three construction
programs sharpen the open questions. At graph level, nineteen exact same-graph min-cut fibers have pairwise disjoint
forward-reachability sets under four directed reductions and bidirectional \(\Delta\)-Y/Y-\(\Delta\) moves, with
symmetric-closure orbit disjointness open. At state level, six exact noninjective fibers of the joint
complete-cut/state map collapse under an exact diagonal internal-bond tensor gauge: on any fixed connected binary-copy
\(C2\_L1\) carrier, equal-product positive capacity assignments are gauge-related up to vertex-wise scalars. Whether
gravitational record formation follows dynamically, and which scalar-dressed invariant candidates persist as physical
records, remain open. Every result is finite and conditional on its declared carrier, caps, and conventions, with
public machine-verifiable validators; no nature-level parameter derivation, universality theorem, or frame transfer to
nature is claimed.
\end{abstract}

\noindent\textbf{Keywords.} Six Birds Theory; emergence calculus; finite structural constructions; Standard Model gauge selection; quantum gravity; holographic entanglement.
