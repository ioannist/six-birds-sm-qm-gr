\hypertarget{cross-track-ties-and-the-one-grammar-thesis}{%
\section{Cross-track comparison: one vocabulary, conditional recurrences}\label{cross-track-ties-and-the-one-grammar-thesis}}

The two tracks use the same formal vocabulary: quotients, closure, factorization defects, common refinements, and typed
landing modes. That reuse is real and useful for organizing finite constructions. Post-publication review does not,
however, support the stronger Version-1 statements that this grammar ``forces'' cross-domain physics, achieves a
foundational grade, or demonstrates universality. Several proposed ties depended on claims now retracted: record-stability
grounding, a forced gravitational record channel, route non-commutation, and one Born--area composition law.

\hypertarget{why-the-ties-are-non-trivial-the-anti-contamination-guard}{%
\subsection{The anti-contamination protocol and its limit}\label{why-the-ties-are-non-trivial-the-anti-contamination-guard}}

The SM construction was typed as a selection/measure layer and the QM--GR construction as a common-refinement/access
problem. Validators rejected certain cross-track tokens, and the build history contains genuine self-demotions. This is
evidence of procedural separation, not mathematical independence or blinding: the protocol was designed and reported by
the same program, the external missing-layer gate never closed, and the atlases are not independent preregistration
evidence. Hence recurrence of a formal object across the tracks is a comparison datum, not proof that nature realizes a
substrate-independent law.

\hypertarget{the-one-grammar-thesis}{%
\subsection{The bounded one-grammar statement}\label{the-one-grammar-thesis}}

The certified cross-cutting claim is therefore:

\begin{quote}
One frozen structural vocabulary was instantiated on two finite, differently typed carriers. It produced several sound
conditional constructions, two genuine toy theorems, and useful diagnostics of where stronger claims failed. The
recurrence is an \(n=2\) methodological datum. It neither demonstrates universality nor transfers any statement to
nature.
\end{quote}

This bound is substantive. It makes the failed predictions informative rather than hiding them: exact quotienting turns
the published P1 kernel into gauge, while P1's two finite subproblems are completed and retyped. The graph computation
establishes forward-reachability disjointness for nineteen exact fibers but leaves symmetric-closure orbit disjointness
open; the six exact joint cut/state-map fibers collapse under the constructed diagonal tensor gauge. Explicit channel
analysis isolates P2's missing dynamical premise, and a broader invariant census shows P3's dependence on record
definition. No bulk-geometry theorem follows, and P2 and P3 remain construction programs requiring gravitational
dynamics and invariant-ring/dynamical record machinery not supplied here.

The campaign, including this version's response to an external adversarial review, is a bounded example of SBT-guided
theory repair: typed failures generated reconstructed carriers, sharper theorem objects, exact countermodels, and
successor construction requirements. It promotes no new physical SM/QM/GR theory; it demonstrates an
emergence-engineering workflow at the level of a research package.

\hypertarget{the-honest-bound-on-the-thesis}{%
\subsection{Limits}\label{the-honest-bound-on-the-thesis}}

\begin{itemize}
\item Both carrier families are finite and deliberately capped; there is no continuum or nature-level frame transfer.
\item The anti-contamination guard is procedural self-report, not independent blinding.
\item Recognition results retain every named import: in particular the regular \(SU(5)\) embedding and the graph/tensor
network model.
\item The campaign completed an adversarial claims pass and produced machine-verifiable repairs; it is not journal review
or independent replication.
\item The missing-layer and unification atlases have unresolved public-support limitations listed in Section 9 and
Appendix B.
\end{itemize}
