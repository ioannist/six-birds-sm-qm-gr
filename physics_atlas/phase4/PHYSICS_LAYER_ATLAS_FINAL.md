## 1. The foreclosure map

The atlas types inter-layer physics edges into four edge-tiers:

- **E0 — run-only**: a constructible run method that EXISTS reads off a non-descending emergent

- **E1 — computable-descent**: a lawful shadow that factors through the right quotient (Ξ→0 on a
  finite toy). Derivable, checkable.
- **E2 — recognition-conditional**: lands only by importing ONE named external principle under a
  stated closure assumption. Never an unconditional derivation.
- **E3 — foreclosed-gap**: located and typed but not closed — no clean descent, no single accepted
  named principle, no constructible run.

The map is **shadow-heavy, emergence-sparse, and foreclosure-dominant**, and this is exactly the
shape SBT's own meta-theory predicts:

- **Shadow-heavy (17 E1).** The overwhelming majority of accepted inter-layer physics is **downward

  (the within-layer-is-empty corollary).

  and turbulence scaling (E031). **Each is a run already routinely done** — proofs-of-concept that an

The asymmetry between **4 E0 (up)** and **17 E1 (down)** is the up/down asymmetry of the primer made
