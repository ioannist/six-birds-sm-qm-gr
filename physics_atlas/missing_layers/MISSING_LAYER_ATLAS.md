# Missing-Layer Atlas — Physics Layer Atlas, Stage 2

**What this is.** Nine edges in the Physics Layer Atlas sealed at **E3** (sharpened gap / foreclosure
no-go). An E3 verdict is **not a dead end**. The decision-procedure that produced it — descent-test
fails, recognition-search finds no single accepted named import, run-search finds no constructible
non-descending run-object — also *locates and types* the obstruction. This atlas reads each E3 backwards:
**SBT has detected that a lawful layer is MISSING between or above the two endpoints, and the same
procedure tells us the SHAPE of the hole.** We characterize that shape rigorously and honestly.

**The discipline (binding, non-negotiable).**
1. **Structural requirement, not content.** We specify the missing layer's required *structure* — its
   lens, the distinctions it must carry, its relation to the endpoints, its role-profile. We **never**
   claim to know the content (the actual new physics). "The hole must be this shape," never "the missing
   theory is X."
2. **No fitting to the conventional candidate.** The received answer ("quantum gravity," "a UV
   completion," "SUSY," "an interpretation") is an *assumption* about the hole's shape. We characterize
   the shape fresh from the endpoints' closure-signatures and the atlas's own corroborating edges, then
   give an honest match / partial / diverges verdict. No forced agreement; no manufactured divergence.
3. **Casting-conditional.** The shape depends on how the endpoints are cast as closures. Every card
   reads: "GIVEN this audited casting, the required shape is X."
4. **Divergence is a flagged hypothesis,** not a proof convention is wrong — sometimes it means the
   *casting* is off, not the physics. Both readings reported.
5. **Every card carries an out-of-sample structural test, or is explicitly marked NOT-YET-GENUINE** — a
   prediction the missing-layer hypothesis makes that was *not* used to build the card. Each test is
   strength-labeled (`genuine_strong` / `genuine_low_strength` / `held_out_but_uncharted` /
   `not_yet_genuine`). **E042 is the sole current `not_yet_genuine` card.**

**Provenance.** All nine gap-edge tiers and modalities, and every corroborating-edge tier/modality cited
below, are confirmed against the authoritative `phase3/scored_run/edge_manifest.jsonl`. Four of the nine
(E018, E019, E020, E021) are **calibration anti-controls** carrying `must_not_seal_E1_or_E2` — the
firewall test that SBT does not secretly derive the gauge group / generations / Λ / quantum gravity. The
calibration passed: all four sealed E3. This means the "missing layer" reading is not a flattering
re-spin of a derivation SBT secretly performed; it is the structural residue of a *forced* foreclosure.

## Executive summary

The nine E3 gaps fall into **three clusters** by shared missing layer, plus one near-singleton. Read as
common-refinement detections (F51 — unification by finding the shared hole, never by derivation), **two
of the clusters are common-refinement detections** — Cluster A a firm one, Cluster B a *specialized
candidate family* (one layer vs several specialized sibling layers unresolved): a single missing layer, if
it existed, would close several gaps at once.

- **Cluster A — the trans-Planckian / pre-geometric layer.** `{E018 (QM↔GR), E042 (GR→singularities),
  E021 (Λ)}` plus E032's speculative shape-B reading. One missing layer above GR (the sought QM↔GR

  distinction — ħ/Planck-scale content co-defined with curvature, non-definable from GR's classical
  lens. **Genuine F51 common-refinement.** E018 is the worked flagship.
- **Cluster B — the SM-structure-selection layer.** `{E019 (gauge group), E020 (generations), E043
  (EW hierarchy), E037 (string→SM)}`. One selection/measure layer above the SM that fixes its free
  structural inputs as *selected* content. E037's residual hole *is* the measure that the other three
  need. **Candidate F51 common-refinement — a *specialized SM-selection family*:** the per-edge predicates
  are *specialized* (gauge-group vs generation-count vs scalar-scale), so it is a *family* of sibling holes
  sharing a parent; **whether *one* layer supplies all of them or several specialized layers do is
  unresolved / contested content**, not an established single-layer unification.
- **Cluster C — the UV-completion-and-selection chain.** `{E009 (EFT→QFT UV completion), E037 (string→SM)}`.
  A single descent chain L → relativistic_qft → rg_eft; the hole is the *inversion* of an existing
  non-invertible shadow (E008), a selector on the UV-completion fiber. E037 is the bridge node shared
  with Cluster B.
- **Near-singleton — the measurement-completion layer.** `{E032 (QM→outcome)}` clusters with the
  *recognition* edges E007 (Born weights) and E026 (pointer basis) as three faces of one
  quantum→definite-fact closure; it is *not* in the open-question structural-selection family.

**Cluster B overlaps Cluster A at E021** (the cosmological constant is both an SM-side scale-selection
puzzle and a GR-side trans-Planckian puzzle). **Cluster C overlaps B at E037.** So the three clusters
form a connected graph meeting at the two "landscape / vacuum-energy" nodes — flagged, not asserted as
one mega-layer.

**The surviving findings (the headline): one firm relocation/sharpening, contested divergences, honest
agreement.** After hardening, the firm finding is a **relocation/sharpening — *not* a divergence from
convention** (serious convention already recognizes the selection/measure problem); only the contested,
