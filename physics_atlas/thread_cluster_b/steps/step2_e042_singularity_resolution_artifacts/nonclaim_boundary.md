This step does not quantize gravity, compute Planck-scale physics, or certify frame transfer.  Continuous, Lorentzian, gauge, diffeomorphism, and physically realistic singularity analyses remain outside this toy.

This step does not assert an unconditional atlas closure.  It shows that, on `L_ext`, the GR non-stabilizing curvature defect can be represented as a finite stabilizing L-readout with controls that discriminate a non-resolving readout from the constructed one.

This step matches the conventional high-level expectation at the structural level only: GR's smooth description reaches a boundary, while a richer layer carries finite content.  The actual mechanism remains unknown content.
