# Step 1 Nonclaim Boundary

This step is a finite-carrier frame.  It does not compute the cosmological constant, derive a value of `Lambda`, or identify a physical vacuum-selection mechanism.

This step does not provide a physical singularity-resolution substrate.  It constructs a finite `d4_subplanck` readout with the structural behavior required by E042: smooth-regime descent and high-curvature non-factorization.

This step reuses the E018 toy package `L`; it does not certify frame transfer to continuous, Lorentzian, gauge, diffeomorphism, or physically realistic carriers.

This step does not claim a new physical law.  It specifies the shape of the E021 and E042 holes on the declared toy: the non-descending readouts, their regimes, their obstruction witnesses, and the smooth-shadow boundary.

This step does not close either atlas target unconditionally.  It establishes the shared-substrate frame for later E042 and E021 constructions.
