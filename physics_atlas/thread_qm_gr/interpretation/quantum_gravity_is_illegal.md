## The claim

## What "quantum gravity" usually means

The conventional quest is for a **single theory that is both quantum and gravitational**: quantize the gravitational/geometric layer so it becomes one quantum theory (string theory, loop quantum gravity, asymptotic safety — all variants of "make geometry quantum"), or otherwise derive/fuse the two into one.

> Distinction that matters: the *illegal* object is "the quantization of gravity into a single quantum-geometric theory." The *legal* object is "quantum matter **sourcing** classical geometry" (semiclassical gravity / QFT-in-curved-spacetime), which works and is empirically successful (Hawking radiation, inflationary perturbations). The framework forbids the former and recovers the latter.

1. **It is a cross-layer derivation, and that is the one move SBT outlaws.** You may *chart, type, recognize* a layer, never *derive* one across a foreclosed boundary. "Quantize gravity" = derive the geometric layer as content of the quantum layer. Forbidden by definition (the anti-reductionism guardrail / no-smuggling gates).
2. **The fusion is not a lawful layer.** When the construction tried the literal "put QM and GR into one package," it failed `G_nosmuggle` — the union just *duplicates* the shared structure; it is not a single closure (Step 17). A theory that *is* both is that illegal union.
3. **There is no single quantum-gravitational audit.** A unified theory needs one currency that is simultaneously the quantum one and the gravitational one. The construction *derived* both from one field and found they **cannot be the same audit** — probability `|ψ|²` and energy `T[ψ]` are different functionals (Step 24). The demand for one shared quantum-gravitational price is unsatisfiable.

## The part that earns the reframe: the technical walls *are* the illegality

The two famous obstacles to quantum gravity —

- **non-renormalizability** (you quantize gravity and it blows up), and
- **the problem of time** (no external clock for a quantum theory of the whole geometry) —

stop being "hard problems we haven't cracked." In the framework they are the **signature of the forbidden move showing up as mathematics**:

- **Non-renormalizability = the P3 route-mismatch**: quantize-then-curve ≠ curve-then-quantize — the derivation does not close *because it is not allowed to*. The two orders fail to commute precisely because neither layer factors through the other.
- **The problem of time = the same illegality on the P2 axis** (the GR-consistent form is *constrained and relational*, not externally timed; resolved here by a Page–Wootters timeless/relational construction).

You do not fix a "forbidden" sign by trying harder. The wall *is* the law being enforced.

The legal structure (quantum matter co-sourcing classical geometry) is exactly the co-sourcing field-layer the framework recovered — one field, read two irreducibly-different ways.
