\paragraph{Step 27 finite constraint statement.}
Let \(\mathcal{H}_c \cong \mathbb{C}^{16}\) be a finite clock register and
let \(\mathcal{H}_f \cong \mathbb{C}^{8}\) be the Step 26 field space at the
self-sourced stationary fixed potential \(V_\ast\).  Let
\[
H_f = \frac12 \Delta_{\mathrm{per}} + \mathrm{diag}(V_\ast)
\]
be the finite Hermitian field Hamiltonian and let \(\psi_\ast\) be the Step
26 stationary field with energy \(E_\ast\).

Choose a finite Hermitian clock generator \(P_c\) with a distinguished clock
history vector
\[
c_t = 16^{-1/2}\exp(-iE_\ast t\,dt)
\]
as an eigenvector of eigenvalue \(-E_\ast\).  Define the clock-field
constraint
\[
C = P_c \otimes I_f + I_c \otimes H_f.
\]
The finite history state
\[
\Psi = \sum_t c_t |t\rangle \otimes \psi_\ast
\]
satisfies \(C\Psi=0\) up to numerical tolerance because
\(P_c c=-E_\ast c\) and \(H_f\psi_\ast=E_\ast\psi_\ast\).

\paragraph{Observed finite-carrier result.}
The compatible constraint has kernel dimension \(1\) at tolerance \(10^{-8}\)
and \(\|C\Psi\|=8.547035876629756\cdot 10^{-16}\).  Conditioning \(\Psi\)
on clock value \(t\) recovers the Step 26 stationary unitary field
\(\exp(-iH_f t\,dt)\psi_\ast\) with maximum direct residual
\(9.79195701998934\cdot 10^{-16}\).

\paragraph{Control.}
Replacing \(P_c\) by the positive clock generator
\((|E_\ast|+0.5)I_c\) gives a constraint with no numerical kernel; its
minimum absolute eigenvalue is \(0.9868198124684662\).  Thus the
empty-kernel control fails the same physical-space test.

\paragraph{Scope.}
The statement is a finite-carrier diagnostic constraint representation of
the Step 26 stationary toy dynamics.  It is not a broad physical adequacy
claim.
