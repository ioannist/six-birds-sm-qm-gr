\paragraph{Step 28 finite non-stationary relational statement.}

let \(\mathcal{H}_f\cong\mathbb{C}^{8}\) be the finite field space.  Starting
from Step 24 sample \(2\), define a self-sourced finite history by

Conditioning on clock value \(t\) recovers \(\psi_t\) with maximum field

open-chain rows.  On the same non-stationary history, its residual is
\(0.0925307478333232\), so the control fails.
