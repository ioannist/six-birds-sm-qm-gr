# Step 26 Nonclaim Boundary

This step does not claim a full physical theory. It constructs and tests a finite toy semiclassical back-reaction loop on the Step 24/25 field carrier.

This step does not claim an unconditional endpoint closure. The result is scoped to one periodic `N=8` finite lattice, one selected initial field, and the declared toy Hamiltonian and stress-energy density.

This step does not derive one endpoint theory from the other. Both endpoint descents remain readouts from the shared field package, and the dynamics is tested only as a package-internal co-sourcing loop.

This step does not replace external review. The finite convergence result only says that the declared moderate toy coupling has a self-consistent field-potential fixed point while the over-strong control fails.

This step does not assert that all couplings converge. The over-strong control demonstrates that the test can fail.

This step does not promote the fixed point to empirical prediction, physical constant, or new law. It is a diagnostic artifact for the construction track.
