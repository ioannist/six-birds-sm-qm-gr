\paragraph{Step 26 finite semiclassical dynamics statement.}
Let \(Z\) be the periodic \(N=8\) lattice carrying a complex field
\(\psi \in \mathbb{C}^{8}\) with \(\|\psi\|_2=1\).  Let
\[
T_{00}(\psi)_i
  =
  |(\nabla_{\mathrm{per}}\psi)_i|^2
  +
  B_i |\psi_i|^2
\]
for the fixed background \(B\) inherited from the Step 24 finite field
carrier.  For a coupling \(\kappa\), define the sourced potential
\[
V_\kappa(\psi)=B+\kappa T_{00}(\psi)
\]
and the Hermitian finite Hamiltonian
\[
H[V] = \frac12 \Delta_{\mathrm{per}} + \mathrm{diag}(V).
\]
The Schrödinger readout \(U[V]\psi=\exp(-iH[V]dt)\psi\) is norm
preserving because \(H[V]\) is Hermitian.

\paragraph{Diagnostic criterion.}
A toy semiclassical self-consistency point is a pair
\((\psi_\ast,V_\ast)\) such that
\[
V_\ast = V_\kappa(\psi_\ast)
\quad\text{and}\quad
H[V_\ast]\psi_\ast = E_\ast\psi_\ast
\]
up to the declared numerical tolerance.  The Step 26 diagnostic iterates
the map
\[
\psi_{m+1}
  =
  \mathrm{norm}\!\left((1-\alpha)\psi_m
    + \alpha\,\phi_0(H[V_\kappa(\psi_m)])\right),
\]
where \(\phi_0\) is the lowest stationary vector of the current Hamiltonian.

\paragraph{Observed finite-carrier result.}
For \(\kappa=0.3,\alpha=0.5\), the fixed-point residual is
\(3.4936318023019015\cdot 10^{-16}\) and the eigen residual is
\(6.377748916558787\cdot 10^{-16}\).  For the over-strong control
\(\kappa=30.0,\alpha=1.0\), the fixed-point residual is
\(1.4285712942487314\) and the eigen residual is \(0.6766429242147017\).
Thus the moderate toy coupling is dynamically self-consistent on this
finite carrier, while the control fails the same test.

\paragraph{Scope.}
The statement is a finite-carrier diagnostic result for the declared toy
dynamics.  It is not a broad physical adequacy result.
