\section*{Step 8 Structural Statement: Run-Level Obstruction for \(G_{v4}\)}

\begin{definition}[Bounded run grammar \(G_{v4}\)]
The grammar \(G_{v4}\) permits a finite stochastic substrate whose sector
readouts are measured after the run.  A candidate run must exhibit a common
completion fixed point and an independent held-out witness.  The grammar
excludes a triple objective in the update rule, the triple target row as an
input, a pairwise-union witness, and post-run diagonal forcing.
\end{definition}

\begin{definition}[Substrate and readouts]
The tested substrate is a binary periodic ring with a stochastic nearest-neighbor
parallel update.  At refinements \(N=32\) and \(N=64\), the run-readouts are:
\[
  A=\text{boundary mutual-information proxy},\quad
  G=\text{first Fourier-mode amplitude proxy},\quad
  R=\text{coarse/evolve route-closure proxy}.
\]
The common fixed-point residual is the Schur residual of the measured plateau
\((A,G,R)\) against the diagonal subspace
\[
  \{(x,x,x): x\in\mathbb{R}\}.
\]
The independent witness is the held-out \(R^2\) gain from adding an interaction
term beyond pairwise predictors.
\end{definition}

\begin{proposition}[Run-level obstruction for the declared substrate]
For the declared run, the normalized fixed-point residuals are
\[
  \Xi_{N=32}=0.4947207905172907,\qquad
  \Xi_{N=64}=0.5371910513210945.
\]
The held-out interaction gains are
\[
  -0.02326224393247789,\qquad -0.08539192789918792.
\]
Thus neither refinement satisfies the declared candidate thresholds
\[
  \Xi \le 0.02,\qquad \Delta R^2_{\mathrm{interaction}}\ge 0.05.
\]
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
The values are computed directly by `simulate_step8_gv4_run.py` from trajectory
windows after burn-in.  The update rule uses only local neighbor coupling and
inertia.  The sector readouts, witness, and common-completion residual are not
arguments of the update rule.  Both refinements have positive residuals far
above the threshold and non-positive held-out witness gains.  The only
near-zero control forces the measured plateau onto the diagonal after the run,
which is excluded by \(G_{v4}\).
\end{proof}

\begin{remark}[Next grammar]
A next attempt must change the substrate/dynamics class or import an external
recognition source with independent records.  It cannot reuse pairwise-union or
post-run diagonal forcing as the witness.
\end{remark}
