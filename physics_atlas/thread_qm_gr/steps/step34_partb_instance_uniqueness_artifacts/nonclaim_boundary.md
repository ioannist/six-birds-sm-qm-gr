# Step 34 Nonclaim Boundary

Step 34 proves bounded-grammar instance-uniqueness only in the precise sense of a unique minimal admissible common refinement up to equivalence on the declared finite coordinate-lattice carrier.

It does not claim `L` is the only admissible object.  The five-mode `M_super_5mode` control is admissible and intentionally shows that super-minimal refinements exist; they are non-minimal because `L` factors through them.

It does not assert absolute physical uniqueness.  The result is scoped to the maps, carrier, admissibility proxy, and quotient-lattice factorization used in this thread.

It does not certify frame transfer to richer carriers, Lorentzian geometry, full Hilbert-space quantum theory, or full gravitational dynamics.  external review remains required.

It does not settle E018 outside the bounded grammar or upgrade the toy construction into a complete physical theory.
