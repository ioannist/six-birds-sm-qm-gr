\section*{Step 7 Structural Statement: Bounded \(G_{v3}\) Triple-Audit No-Go}

\begin{definition}[Bounded grammar \(G_{v3}\)]
The grammar \(G_{v3}\) inherits the filtered rows \(u_i\), \(v\), and \(w\),
which already generate the three pairwise bridge shadows.  A new triple-audit
atom is admissible only if it has independently declared witness content and
generates the triple row as a shadow.  The grammar excludes the native triple
row, the pairwise-union row, and a primitive package-coherence stipulation.
\end{definition}

\begin{definition}[Carrier and target]
At refinement level \(r\in\{2,4\}\), let the carrier have basis
\[
  u_1,\ldots,u_r,\quad v,\quad w,\quad t,\quad s,
\]
with \(C=I\).  The inherited native lens is
\[
  L_{uvw}=\{u_1,\ldots,u_r,v,w\}.
\]
The remaining target bridge is
\[
  B_T=t.
\]
The row \(s\) is an independent witness-control coordinate with no audited map
to \(t\).
\end{definition}

\begin{proposition}[Local saturation of \(G_{v3}\)]
For \(r=2,4\), the accepted \(G_{v3}\) search leaves
\[
  \frac{\tr\,\Xi_I(B_T\mid L_{uvw})}{\tr K_{B_TB_T}}=1.0.
\]
The coupled target residual remains
\[
  \tr\,\Xi_I=1.0,\qquad
  \frac{\tr\,\Xi_I}{\tr K_{DD}}=\frac{1}{7}=0.14285714285714285.
\]
Adding a row in \(\mathrm{span}(L_{uvw})\) does not change the residual.  Adding
\(s\) without an audited map to \(t\) also does not change the residual.
Adding \(t\) itself makes the residual vanish, but that row is excluded by the
grammar.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
The triple row \(t\) is orthogonal to the span of \(u_i\), \(v\), and \(w\).
Therefore the Schur residual of \(t\) relative to \(L_{uvw}\) is its full
unit trace.  A pairwise-union row lies in the same span, so it cannot alter the
projection of \(t\).  The independent row \(s\) is orthogonal to \(t\), and
without an audited map \(s\mapsto t\), it contributes no projection onto the
target row.  The only zero-control adds \(t\) itself, which is precisely the
excluded target-row insertion.  Hence \(G_{v3}\), as declared, is saturated
with the triple residual still present.
\end{proof}

\begin{remark}[Structurally different next approach]
A next approach must supply a run-generated or externally recognized triple
mechanism with independent record content.  It cannot be another native copy of
\(t\), and it cannot be the union of the three pairwise bridge atoms.
\end{remark}
