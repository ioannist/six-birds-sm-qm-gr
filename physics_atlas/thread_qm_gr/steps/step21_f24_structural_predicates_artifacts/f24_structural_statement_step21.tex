\paragraph{Step 21 structural F24 selectors.}
Let \(H\) be the finite carrier, \(q:H\to Q\) the current access
quotient, and \(s:H\to S\) the role readout.  For finite readouts
\(u:H\to U\) and \(v:H\to V\), write
\[
  u\preceq v
  \quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
  \forall h,h'\in H,\ v(h)=v(h')\Rightarrow u(h)=u(h').
\]
Thus \(u\preceq v\) says that \(u\) factors through \(v\).

The Step 21 predicates are structural predicates on a candidate record
\(C\):
\[
\begin{array}{ll}
\mathrm{Mem}(C) &\iff s\preceq m_C \text{ and } m_C\npreceq q,\\
\mathrm{Hid}(C) &\iff s\preceq \ell_C,\ \ell_C\npreceq q,\text{ and no admissible explicit }J_C,\\
\mathrm{Med}(C) &\iff J_C \text{ exists, } a_Cj_C=q,\ r_Cj_C=s,\text{ gates pass, and }j_C\text{ strictly refines }q,\\
\mathrm{Bud}(C) &\iff \Xi_C>0 \text{ is priced and adequately statused, with no exact resolution},\\
\mathrm{Scp}(C) &\iff \mathcal O_s(H)\ne\emptyset \text{ and }\mathcal O_s(H_C)=\emptyset\text{ for a proper }H_C\subset H,\\
\mathrm{Crs}(C) &\iff \mathcal O_{\bar s_C}(H)=\emptyset \text{ for a changed coarsened readout }\bar s_C,\\
\mathrm{Out}(C) &\iff s \text{ is named outside the declared access scope},\\
\mathrm{Blk}(C) &\iff \text{no prior structural predicate fires and closure formation or residual status fails}.
\end{array}
\]
