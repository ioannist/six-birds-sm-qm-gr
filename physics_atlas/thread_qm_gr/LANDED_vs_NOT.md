## The one rule that removes the fog

A question is **LANDED** iff we answer it **without smuggling the answer in.** Honest ways to land one — all real
answers, not consolation prizes:

- **COMPUTE** — a number/relation/verdict drops out of the construction.
- **GROUND** *(conditional)* — a condition not derivable *within its own layer* is provided as the **down-shadow of a
  declared higher recognition source** (relocation up the tower). This **is** a landing, with two tracked caveats:
  (i) source-warrant, (ii) theorem-strength (recognition-source vs proved). (Same mode as the SM track's
  `LANDED_vs_NOT.md`.)
- **RECOGNITION-LANDING** — a *known* result is shown to be a special case / forced instance of an SBT law, **non-
  circularly** (the no-shortcut is the certificate). Recovery, not new physics — but a real landing.
- **FORBIDDEN-RULE / DEMARCATION** — the framework *forbids* a configuration or *classifies* a quantity as
  law-fixable vs contingent, computed with a can-fail control. A falsifiable yes/no.

Ways to **NOT land** one: **SMUGGLE-ONLY** (answering it re-imports the answer — faking it is smuggling) ·
**OUT-OF-SCOPE** (needs frame-transfer / dynamics / an experiment we don't model). One non-answer: **NAMED RESIDUAL**
(an honestly-flagged open frontier that could even go the other way).

**The smuggles that *would* have faked landings were found and deleted:** the area-as-cell-count tautology (Steps 39/40),
the δS relabeling (Step 43), the von-Neumann=Shannon tautology (Step 50 first pass), the circular/hardcoded F51 forcing
(Step 51 first pass). **So nothing in the LANDED column is faked.**
