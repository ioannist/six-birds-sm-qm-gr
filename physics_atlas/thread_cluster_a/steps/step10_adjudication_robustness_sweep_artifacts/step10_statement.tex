\section*{Bounded Grade}

This is a finite-grammar robustness sweep for the Step 6 E043 degeneracy audit. It tests whether the qualitative collapse-vs-landscape adjudication is stable across changes in threshold, width schedules, and grid resolution. It does not compute a physical value or settle a real mechanism.

\section*{Sweep}

The sweep covers 9520 cells:

\begin{itemize}
\item significance thresholds from 0.02 to 0.80,
\item broad final widths from 1.00 to 4.00,
\item sharp final widths from 0.02 to 1.00,
\item the exact Step 6 grid plus log and linear grids at 6, 12, 24, and 48 points over two ranges.
\end{itemize}

A cell holds when the broad family remains multiply supported and the sharp family collapses to one significant point.

\section*{Step 6 Operating Point}

The Step 6 point is threshold 0.25, broad final width 2.00, sharp final width 0.10 on the exact Step 6 grid. It is classified as \texttt{VERDICT\_HOLDS}. Its broad sequence is \texttt{6->6->6->6->6->6}; its sharp sequence is \texttt{3->2->2->1->1}.

The nearest same-grid flip is a \texttt{sharp\_noncollapse} cell at the same threshold and broad width but sharp final width 0.40. The normalized margin to that flip is 0.306122448980.

\section*{Flip Boundaries}

Two flip families occur:

\begin{itemize}
\item \texttt{sharp\_noncollapse}: wide-sharp or low-threshold regimes where the sharp family remains multiply supported; 3990 cells.
\item \texttt{broad\_false\_collapse}: high-threshold, coarse-grid, or narrow-broad regimes where the broad family is counted as a singleton; 608 cells.
\end{itemize}

\section*{Verdict}

The Step 6 adjudication is robust across an explicitly mapped region of the finite toy grammar, and the Step 6 operating point is not a one-cell edge case. The result remains bounded by the declared finite sweep and its mapped flip boundaries.
