\section*{Bounded Grade}

This is a finite-grammar order battery for the Cluster-A selection toy. It tests whether the collapse-vs-landscape distinction depends on a hand-aimed refinement order, using the Step-8 token-blind structural survivor carrier.

\section*{Carrier and Measures}

The carrier is the 468-world structural survivor set from Step 8. The seven facets are \texttt{gauge\_code}, \texttt{rep\_code}, \texttt{n\_gen}, \texttt{texture\_code}, \texttt{ew\_code}, \texttt{uv\_code}, and \texttt{vacuum\_code}.

The genuine-selection case is a sharp structural measure centered on the structural argmax, selected without token names. The landscape case is a broad flat support over the same partial-refinement cylinder. Each order reveals four facets, not all seven.

\section*{Order Ensemble}

The order ensemble contains:

\begin{itemize}
\item the canonical order,
\item the reverse order,
\item 200 fixed-seed random orders with seed 20260606,
\item an adversarial order chosen to delay genuine collapse,
\item an adversarial order chosen to force landscape collapse.
\end{itemize}

\section*{Results}

\begin{center}
\begin{tabular}{lrrrr}
\toprule
Case & Random Orders & Collapse Fraction & Non-collapse Fraction & Final Range \\
\midrule
Genuine selection & 200 & 1.000000000000 & 0.000000000000 & 1--1 \\
Landscape measure & 200 & 0.000000000000 & 1.000000000000 & 6--27 \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}

The adversarial delay-genuine order gives genuine final degeneracy 1 and landscape final degeneracy 27. The adversarial force-landscape order gives genuine final degeneracy 1 and landscape final degeneracy 6.

Across all 204 orders, the max genuine final degeneracy is 1 and the min landscape final degeneracy is 6, giving separation margin 5. No tested order flips either verdict.

\section*{Verdict}

On this declared finite battery, the collapse-vs-landscape distinction is order-invariant. The result answers the hand-aimed-refinement concern at toy fidelity: the verdict is driven by the measure structure, not by the chosen order.

This remains a bounded toy result. It does not compute physical values or settle real mechanisms.
