- a physical unification theorem;
- a derivation of measured gauge couplings;
- a derivation of the real Standard Model;
- a new physical theory;
- a frame-transfer status upgrade;
- a proof that the finite balance rule is the real physical mechanism.

Remaining inputs are explicit: the Step-23 finite closure criteria, bounded factor/rank range, and the trace-zero balance rule. The result is a finite-carrier hardening of the Mode-B construction, not a physical law certificate.
