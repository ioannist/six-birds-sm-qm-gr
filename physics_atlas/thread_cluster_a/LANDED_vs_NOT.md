## The one rule that removes the fog

A question is **LANDED** iff we answer it **without smuggling the answer in.** Four honest ways to land one —
all four are *real answers*, not consolation prizes:

- **SELECT** — the machinery picks it out, resting on conditions we **declare**.
- **PROVE-BLIND** — we **prove** the framework cannot determine it ⇒ it is observed-input *by proof* (a positive result).
- **COMPUTE** — a number/relation drops out of the construction.
- **GROUND** *(conditional)* — it is not derivable *within its own layer*, so we **provide it as the down-shadow
  of a declared higher recognition source** (relocation up the tower). This **is** a landing, not a hedge — but a
  **conditional** one, and we tag it as such. Every GROUND verdict carries **two separately-tracked caveats:**
  (i) its **source-warrant** — the higher source's own justification relocates further up the tower (externally
  judged at the top); and (ii) its **theorem-strength** — enumeration-robust vs proved. So GROUND is **not
  epistemically identical** to SELECT/COMPUTE/PROVE-BLIND, which close *inside* the layer. We nonetheless hold it
  a real landing, because in SBT *every* condition is supplied by a recognition source somewhere — SELECT itself
  rests on a **declared** condition, and a finite tower always bottoms out at an external top — so **grounding a
  previously-bare stipulation in a higher source is an upgrade**, not a derivation-from-nothing. (N_gen-blindness
  was a real landing on enumeration before its Step-62 theorem; same shape.) Read every GROUND row *with* its
  conditional tag.

Two honest ways to **NOT land** one:

- **SMUGGLE-ONLY** — answering it requires re-importing the answer. Faking it = smuggling.
- **OUT-OF-SCOPE** — it needs a construction we deliberately excluded (the E0 emergence run) or an experiment.

One non-answer: **REFRAME** — we restate it; we do not solve it.

The smuggles that *would* have faked landings were found and deleted: the `total_slots=5` prior, the
shape-flavored `factor_local_breaking`, the minimality `currency` rescue, the SU(2)-cubic bug. **So nothing in
the LANDED column is faked, and nothing in the NOT-LANDED column is dressed up.**
