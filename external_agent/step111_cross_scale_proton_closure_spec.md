# Step111 — Cross-scale proton carrier closure

## Status

This specification has two separately typed deliverables.

1. **step111a: exact invariant-graph demonstrator** — complete and executable.  It closes the logical gap in step110 by showing, with exact algebra and a contraction certificate, how a coupling that is arbitrary at one scale becomes unique once the refinement/package law is imposed.
2. **step111b: 2+1-flavor SU(3) production run** — the concrete QCD construction that replaces the demonstrator's frozen rational cell by a locally generated gauge–Grassmann block tensor.  Only step111b is licensed to emit `m_N/m_Omega`.

The distinction is a no-smuggling requirement.  A physical ratio may not be attached to the step110 rational matrix or to a hand-written RG map.

## 1. The missing mathematical object: an invariant graph

Split the carrier coordinates at scale `k` into

- `x_k`: relevant and marginal coordinates fixed by the local QCD grammar and the independently declared calibration channels;
- `y_k`: promoted hidden-channel coordinates, including the `qqq <-> qqqg` coupling that appeared as the free scalar `s` in step110.

A generated refinement step has the form

\[
 x_{k+1}=\Sigma(x_k),\qquad
 y_{k+1}=G(x_k,y_k).
\]

Cross-scale closure is not the statement `y=G(y)` at an arbitrary scale.  It is the invariant-graph equation

\[
 h(\Sigma x)=G(x,h(x)).
\]

If `G` is uniformly contractive in its second argument,

\[
 \|G(x,y)-G(x,y')\|\le q\|y-y'\|,\qquad q<1,
\]

then the carrier has a unique invariant graph `y=h(x)`.  This is the precise mechanism by which the single-scale freedom disappears.  The coefficients of `G` must be generated by local block composition; choosing them to obtain a desired mass is forbidden.

Asymptotic freedom supplies the ultraviolet trajectory and dimensional transmutation supplies an RG invariant scale.  The ultraviolet fixed point itself is `g=0`; `Lambda_QCD` is an invariant of the trajectory, not a nonzero fixed-point coupling.  For `m_N/m_Omega`, the overall scale cancels.  The required output is the infrared carrier-root ratio on the unique renormalized trajectory after the two quark-mass coordinates have been calibrated independently.

## 4. The three refinement maps

### `R`: spacing/block refinement

For each channel `H in {pi,K,Omega,N}`, contract a `2^4` block exactly and package it:

\[
\mathcal R_{H,k}
=\Pi_{H,k+1}\circ\mathcal B_k\circ\iota_{H,k}.
\]

- `iota` inserts the promoted carrier into the local boundary tensor;
- `B` performs only local block contractions;
- `Pi` is the semantic predictive/recombination package, fixed without consulting the nucleon root.

### `V`: volume refinement

Tile carrier blocks spatially and require the finite-volume correction coordinate to close under `L -> 2L`.  No global Monte Carlo ensemble is generated.

### `F`: representation/Grassmann refinement

Embed rung `k` into `k+1` by enlarging the SU(3) representation set and retained Grassmann boundary sectors.  The embedding is exact on the old package and supplies a cutoff residual on the new complement.

## 7. Held-out tests

Construction uses rungs `k=0,1,2,3`.  Before opening the nucleon comparator, freeze all code and predict:

1. **Scale holdout:** complete `N` and `Omega` carrier tensors at rung `k=4`.
2. **Volume holdout:** the `L -> 2L` change at a volume not used in `V` construction.
3. **Cutoff holdout:** the next SU(3)-representation/Grassmann rung.
4. **Transport holdout:** two- and three-step baryon responses not used to construct `T`.
5. **Seed independence:** distinct admissible initial hidden-channel coordinates converge to the same invariant graph.

### Knockouts

- erase `C_lambda` or set the crossed `qqq <-> C_rho` seed to zero;
- replace the crossed `F_12->23` action by the aligned identity basis;
- quotient the `qqqg` fibers back to the branchwise product;
- allow `s_k` to float independently at every scale.

At least one commutation residual must cease contracting or the held-out nucleon transport must fail.  The Omega calibration channel must remain stable enough to show that the failure is proton-carrier-specific.
