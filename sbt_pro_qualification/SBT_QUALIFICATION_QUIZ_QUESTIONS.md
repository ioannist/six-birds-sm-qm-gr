## Purpose

This quiz checks whether an agent is sufficiently grounded to reason or build inside Six Birds Theory (SBT) after a context reset.

It is **not authority**. The authority order is:

1. the SBT papers;
2. the running engine and audited code;
3. author-set framework identity and thesis;
4. grounding and memory syntheses;
5. this quiz.

Where a synthesis conflicts with a paper or running code, the paper or code governs. Where the question concerns the framework’s identity or overarching thesis, the author’s settled statement governs, while theorem-grade claims remain limited to what the relevant paper proves.

## Evidence grades to use in answers

Use these labels when relevant:

- `[DEF]` — definition in a paper.
- `[THM]` — theorem proved under stated assumptions.
- `[SCOPED RESULT]` — formal or empirical result limited to a named construction.
- `[PRIMER]` — orientation statement.
- `[CODE FACT]` — fact about the running implementation.
- `[AUTHOR THESIS]` — settled framework-level identity or thesis supplied by the author.
- `[PROJECT HYP]` — current research hypothesis.
- `[WORKFLOW]` — operating discipline.
- `[RETRACTED]` — known wrong conclusion retained only as a warning.

## How to use the quiz

- Questions marked **★** form the critical cold-start gate. Answer those before doing SBT work.
- A critical answer that collapses SBT into ordinary reduction, makes event obstruction the only strict test, or makes the promoted carrier decorative is a stop condition: reread the primer, Foundations I, the strict-extension papers, Holonomy, Promotion/Exactification, and SAU.
- For the full qualification, answer all 80 questions without consulting the answer key.
- Do not give slogans only. State the type of claim, the relevant object, and the guardrail that prevents overclaiming.
