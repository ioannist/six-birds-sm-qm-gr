## Abstract

The title inverts the proverb deliberately: stones cannot be killed, and no open problem of physics is closed here. What is delivered is a common finite constructive grammar -- six structural primitives -- instantiated and stress-tested across three problem domains, with every claim bounded by an explicit verification ledger.

This corrected version reports what survived a post-publication campaign of 27 adversarial review rounds and 20 machine-verifiable repair or probe constructions. The two tracks retain sound finite constructions and two genuine toy theorems, but no reviewed unit warrants a foundational grade or nature-level claim. On the Standard-Model track, a conjugation-consistent finite carrier yields a branch-typed selection: within the declared representation, charge, field, and singleton/two-scalar caps, 2|3 is the unique declared carrier orbit/dimension family admitting a clean breaking branch under the inherited SU(N)->SU(N-1) residual proxy, while every SU(4) branch is breaking. Exact construction of the regular SU(5) embedding derives its one-dimensional hypercharge direction and recovers sin^2(theta_W)=3/8 and k_Y=5/3, conditional on the embedding, 5-bar+10 package, and weak-pair normalization; this is not a low-energy prediction, and Pati--Salam also yields 3/8. On the QM--GR track, contracted-state entropies, graph cuts, monogamy controls, and an explicit max-flow/min-cut dual certificate support a finite-graph recognition of the corrected identity Area(min cut)=sum_e c_e y_e, with y_e the edge shadow prices.

Verification also changed the conclusions materially. The published route-mismatch completions commute; the forcing claims attached to the three predictions are retracted or reduced; the generation-blindness theorem, a universal Born--area composition law, the product-parent 3/23 control, the linearized-Einstein match, and the apparent cosmological-background admissibility boundary do not survive as stated. What remains includes conditional finite constructions, recognition landings, a witness-independent single-factor toy theorem, and several strengthened repaired computations. Version 4 responds to an external adversarial review, with all validators public. At graph level, for each of nineteen base/perturbed pairs, a queue-exhaustive forward search under four directed reductions and bidirectional Delta-Y/Y-Delta finds the two reachability sets disjoint; this establishes forward-reachability disjointness only, and symmetric-closure orbit disjointness remains open. At state level, six positive-algebraic same-graph pairs are exact noninjective fibers of the joint complete-cut/state map, but they collapse under the constructed exact diagonal internal-bond tensor gauge: on any fixed connected binary-copy C2_L1 carrier, equal-product positive capacity assignments are gauge-related up to vertex-wise scalars. No physically complete bulk gauge relation or bulk-geometry underdetermination theorem is established. The other two programs -- whether gravitational record formation follows dynamically, and which scalar-dressed invariant candidates persist as physical records -- remain open. The repository records the authoritative claims registry, repair artifacts, conditions, and validators. No frame-transfer to nature or universality theorem is claimed.
